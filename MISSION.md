# Mission: Junior QA Engineer

## Why

Shivani is a Healthcare Support Worker (Band 2) at Royal Surrey County Hospital who wants to switch into IT as a Junior QA Engineer. Her husband Khushal is a Senior QA Analyst, giving her both household exposure to the field and a built-in mentor. She needs to go from no formal IT background to confidently passing Junior QA interviews and landing her first role — so she can build a career in tech from within the UK.

## Success looks like

- Can explain QA fundamentals (SDLC, STLC, testing types, bug life cycle) fluently in an interview
- Can write professional test cases and bug reports from scratch
- Can use JIRA to raise and track defects
- Can test a REST API using Postman and explain what she tested
- Can describe Agile/Scrum and her role as a QA within a sprint
- Passes a Junior QA take-home test or technical screen with confidence
- Can articulate how her healthcare background is an asset in QA (process discipline, documentation, patient-safety mindset)

## Constraints

- No prior IT or coding background
- Learning alongside a full-time healthcare job
- Husband (Senior QA) available as a real-world sounding board
- Wants to be interview-ready within 6–8 weeks

## Out of scope

- Test automation / coding (Selenium, Python) — defer until 6+ months into first QA role
- Performance testing tools (JMeter, k6) — too advanced for now
- DevOps / CI-CD pipelines — background knowledge only, no hands-on needed yet
- Mobile testing deep-dive — mention in lessons but don't focus on it
